### 🌿 About me

I'm an environmental researcher with **six years of climate and environmental research** behind me, including **more than two years in monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL)**. I work at the **River and Delta Research Centre (RDRC)** in Dhaka, turning field evidence into practical policy for climate, water and sustainable development.

> *"Wetlands aren't restored from offices or spreadsheets. They're restored by the people who live beside them, and by evidence that listens to them."*

### 🔭 What I'm working on

- **[Re-WET](https://rewet.info)**: restoring Korail lake, an urban wetland in Dhaka, through community-led, nature-based solutions. I'm the project's **MEL Officer**. *(UK FCDO · REDAA programme · managed by IIED)*
- Research on **river rights, tree logging, river drying and waterlogging** across Bangladesh at RDRC.

### 🧭 Research interests

`Climate change adaptation` `Wetlands & river governance` `Water resource management` `Fishing communities & river livelihoods` `Environmental migration` `Human–environment geography` `GIS & remote sensing` `Forests & biodiversity` `Environmental policy`
